Short Bio

Dr. Sachin Kumar is an Associate Professor in the Computer Science Department at the Akian College of Science and Engineering, American University of Armenia, since July 2024. He holds a Ph.D. in Data Mining from Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (2017) and has extensive research experience spanning machine learning, artificial intelligence, healthcare informatics, and cybersecurity.

With an H-index of 42 (Google Scholar) and over 60 peer-reviewed publications, Dr. Kumar is recognized as a highly cited researcher, ranking in the top 2% worldwide in 2023-2025 (Stanford University). His research has been supported by multiple research grants including the Russian Science Foundation and Ministry of Science and Education Russian Federation.

Dr. Kumar specializes in Business Analytics, Financial Data Analysis, Smart Healthcare, Cognitive Science, Quantum Machine Learning, and Multimodal Medical Diagnosis. He has supervised postdoctoral fellows, graduate students, and undergraduate researchers, with several students' work published in top-tier venues including Nature Scientific Reports, IEEE, and Springer.
