Active Research Projects (2024-2026)

Lightweight AI for Early Detection of Cognitive Decline
Project Lead: Dr. Sachin Kumar | Student: Arman Manukyan (MS Thesis)
Developing efficient, resource-constrained AI models for early-stage cognitive impairment detection using multimodal brain imaging and clinical data. Focus on edge deployment for healthcare IoT devices.
Key Objectives:
  • Design lightweight neural network architectures for embedded medical devices
  • Integrate multimodal data from MRI, PET, and clinical assessments
  • Develop interpretable models for clinical decision support
  • Validate on real clinical datasets
Status: Ongoing | Expected Outcome: IEEE Access publication (submitted)
Transformer-based Multimodal Medical Diagnosis Framework
Project Lead: Dr. Sachin Kumar | Collaborators: Dr. Sita Saini (Postdoc)
Developing advanced transformer-based architectures for multimodal medical diagnosis, particularly focusing on tuberculosis classification using heterogeneous medical imaging modalities and clinical records.
Key Contributions:
  • Novel attention mechanisms for cross-modal fusion
  • Hierarchical transformer architecture for medical data
  • Explainability through attention visualization
  • Multi-center validation across international datasets
Publications: Journal of Big Data (2025), Bioengineering (2024)
Security and Cryptographic Protocols for Internet of Medical Things (IoMT)
Project Lead: Dr. Sachin Kumar | Researcher: Dr. Anuj Kumar Singh (Postdoc)
Designing and implementing secure, blockchain-integrated authentication and encryption protocols for wearable medical devices and IoMT ecosystems with focus on privacy preservation and regulatory compliance (HIPAA).
Research Focus:
  • Lightweight elliptic curve cryptography for resource-constrained devices
  • Blockchain-based audit trails for medical data
  • Zero-knowledge proofs for privacy-preserving authentication
  • Real-world deployment in healthcare networks
Publications: Scientific Reports (2025), Expert Systems with Applications (2024)
Wireless Sensor Networks: Data Compression and Optimization
Project Lead: Dr. Sachin Kumar | Former Researcher: Dr. Shylashree N. (Postdoc)
Developing advanced autoencoder-based compression techniques for wireless sensor networks, balancing data fidelity, energy consumption, and reconstruction error for IoT environmental monitoring systems.
Achievements:
  • Novel combined compression-encryption framework using autoencoders
  • Dynamic sensor scheduling algorithms
  • Up to 70% data size reduction with minimal reconstruction error
  • Energy-efficient sensor cloud systems
Publications: Scientific Reports (2025), Wireless Personal Communications (2024)
