Research Environment & Philosophy

My research group focuses on creating an inclusive, collaborative, and intellectually stimulating environment where:

  • Excellence is Pursued: We aim for high-quality research published in top-tier venues (Nature, IEEE, Springer, etc.)
  • Innovation is Encouraged: Novel ideas are welcomed; failure is seen as a learning opportunity
  • Mentorship is Prioritized: Personalized guidance for career development and research skills
  • Collaboration is Valued: Both internal team collaborations and external partnerships with international institutions
  • Communication Matters: Regular group meetings, paper discussions, and feedback sessions
